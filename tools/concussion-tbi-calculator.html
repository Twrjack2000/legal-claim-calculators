How Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI) & Concussion Claims Are Valued

Traumatic Brain Injuries—even those initially labeled "mild concussion" in an emergency room—frequently result in life-altering neurological deficits known as Persistent Post-Concussion Syndrome (PCS). Victims suffer from chronic intractable migraines, vestibular vertigo, convergence insufficiency (vision tracking problems), tinnitus, short-term memory loss, executive dysfunction, and personality changes.

Overcoming the "Normal CT Scan" Defense: Standard hospital CT scans are designed only to detect emergency surgical bleeds or skull fractures; they cannot see axonal shearing in the brain's white matter tracts. When a brain injury specialist documents your injury using Diffusion Tensor Imaging (DTI), Susceptibility Weighted Imaging (SWI), Videonystagmography (VNG), and a Neuropsychological Testing Battery, settlement values routinely increase by 300% to 500%.
